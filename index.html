Master's thesis

CPS-Based Smart Adaptive Energy Management Framework for Improving Solar Energy Utilization in Gaza Strip

This research proposes a CPS-based framework combining IoT monitoring and AI-based forecasting to manage residential solar PV energy adaptively. It integrates solar generation and demand forecasting, battery-condition assessment, load prioritization, and smart load switching to improve use of available solar energy under changing, resource-constrained conditions.

Expected contributions include adaptive decision-making, a lightweight AI-CPS design, and evaluation through MATLAB/Simulink simulation and an ESP32-based prototype. These are proposed contributions and expected outcomes, not yet validated results.
